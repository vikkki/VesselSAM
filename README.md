# VesselSAM: A Parameter-Efficient Adaptation of SAM to Aortic Vessel Segmentation with AtrousLoRA

Segmenting the aorta and its branches in CTA requires preserving progressively tapering vessels and irregular boundaries across substantial variations in vessel caliber. The Segment Anything Model (SAM) provides strong pre-trained representations, but adapting it presents a trade-off. Full encoder fine-tuning updates many parameters, whereas conventional Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) reduces the trainable subset but does not aggregate image context across different receptive fields. We present VesselSAM, a parameter-efficient adaptation of SAM for aortic vessel segmentation. Its core module, AtrousLoRA, places ASPP-based atrous attention within LoRA's low-rank residual path, allowing features from multiple dilation rates to be aggregated and spatially weighted in the task-specific update. We evaluate VesselSAM on the multi-center Aortic Vessel Tree (AVT) dataset and the Type-B Aortic Dissection (TBAD) dataset. VesselSAM achieves Dice similarity coefficient (DSC) values of 93.50\%, 93.25\%, and 93.02\% on the three AVT cohorts and 93.26\% on TBAD. Relative to MedSAM-FT, the strongest SAM-based baseline, the DSC gains range from 1.01 to 2.90 percentage points. VesselSAM also achieves the lowest Hausdorff distance among the evaluated ViT- and SAM-based methods across all four cohorts. Training updates 6.8 million parameters, or 7.2\% of the model parameters. These results show that AtrousLoRA can adapt SAM to complex vascular anatomy through a spatially informed low-rank update.
